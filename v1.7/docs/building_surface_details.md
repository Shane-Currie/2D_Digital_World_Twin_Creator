# Building facade and roof details

Generic wall tiles now have one small window per bay rather than two large windows or an uninterrupted glass wall. Matching blank brick/render/glass-panel tiles replace every complete bay touched by a door. Doors are drawn as part of the building pass, aligned to that facade's ground baseline and roof-rise direction, before the roof. This is cached procedural composition, not destructive editing of uploaded images. Door height still targets 115% of the production character, limited by available facade space.

Generic roof textures no longer bake in an air-conditioning unit or chimney that can be stretched and cut by an irregular OSM outline. A separate 1.8 × 2.0 metre cooling-unit picture, including its shadow, is placed toward the camera-facing front roof edge. The complete rectangle plus a 0.3-metre margin must fit the visible roof union, excluding courtyards. If nothing fits, equipment is omitted, never cropped. Custom uploaded roof pictures remain untouched; artwork already baked into them cannot be automatically separated.

Buildings without saved doors receive one decorative door where a clear street/footpath-facing approach is found. Search uses spatially indexed ground-level routes, expanding locally from 20 to 50 to 100 metres and checking up to 64 ordered approach candidates per radius. Bridge/tunnel approaches, buildings and water are excluded. Exact segment-edge checks catch thin obstacles missed by sampling. No town-specific IDs or LLM calls are used. Back-facing doors can be hidden by the fixed camera. Missing routes, obstructed buildings and tiny edges can leave a building unresolved; use Building Creator for intentional placement. This does not assert surveyed entrances or create playable interiors.

Saved doors and their interaction coordinates are preserved. Only doors linked by the runtime to a playable interior receive a green arrow. Arrows are more than twice their previous width, have a tail, and gently move toward the entrance over a 1.4-second cycle. A separate child overlay redraws the small indicators without resubmitting the entire town each animation frame.
