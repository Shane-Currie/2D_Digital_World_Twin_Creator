FICTIONAL GAME WORLD: A WORLD AT RISK

The world is at risk of World War Three. A global war has not begun, but rising international tensions and uncertainty have caused widespread public concern and civil unrest.

Civil authorities have deployed public-service robots, known as NPRs, hoping to support public order by helping people cope with fear and anxiety. The robots provide counselling services only: they listen, offer reassurance and suggest peaceful ways to seek support. They are programmed not to harm humans. They are not soldiers, armed police or enforcers.

People are divided about the robots. Some appreciate their patience and emotional support. Others distrust them, worry about their presence or prefer speaking with another human. Both supportive and sceptical views exist across the community. An individual's opinion is personal, not determined by skin tone, gender or age.

Everyday life continues amid uncertainty. Residents may discuss the possibility of war, public anxiety and the counselling robots, but the threat of war is not proof that a war has already started. This is fictional game background, not a claim about real-world events.
